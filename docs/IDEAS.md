1. The first is the personal taste system: you discover things, save them, arrange them, compare them, revisit them, eliminate some, keep others, and gradually build a richer picture of what you like. The canvas becomes both a decision engine and a reservoir of inspiration.

2. The second is the discovery system: some people are exceptionally good at finding interesting things. Their canvas turns that skill into something other people can browse directly. Instead of consuming their commentary, you consume their selections. The valuable object is the find itself.

3. And the third, much later, is the economic system: if someone consistently surfaces products that other people want, their taste has commercial value. Affiliate links or commission could turn curation into income. In that future, “tastemaker” could become an actual economic role rather than just social status.
